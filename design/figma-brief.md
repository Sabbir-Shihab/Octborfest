```
Design a high-fidelity desktop (1440×900) + mobile (390×844) single-page website called "Ofrenda" — a digital Día de los Muertos remembrance altar for the theme "Ghost in the Machine".

BRAND RULE (critical):
In the first viewport, the word "Ofrenda" must be the dominant hero signal — huge display type (~120px desktop), not a small nav logo. If you remove the top nav, the page must still clearly be Ofrenda. Do not let any other headline overpower the brand.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Atmosphere: deep midnight navy / near-black night sky (#080C18 → #0B1224), soft amber candlelight glow from above, scattered tiny stars, a few out-of-focus marigold ember orbs, falling marigold petals.
- Accent colors: marigold gold #FFB703, warm orange #F4A261, coral #E76F51, crimson #C1121F, teal #2A9D8F, ivory #F8F1E3, soft sand text #CBB89A, wood shelf #5C3D24 → #3A2718.
- Mood: reverent candlelit ofrenda — NOT purple SaaS gradients, NOT cream newspaper layouts, NOT generic white cards in the hero, NOT neon cyberpunk glow spam.
- Motifs: red papel picado scalloped banner, wooden altar shelf with 5 lit candles of different heights/colors (crimson, marigold, ivory, teal, marigold), 3 marigold flowers under the candles.

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Display / brand / headlines: Fraunces (or similar soft serif) — elegant, high contrast.
- UI / body / nav: Sora (or similar geometric sans).
- Nav links: small uppercase tracking, sand color.

LAYOUT — exactly these sections, one job each:

1) TOP NAV (thin)
   - Left: wordmark "Ofrenda" in cream serif
   - Right: DEDICATE · ALTAR

2) HERO (full first viewport, one composition)
   - Left: giant brand "Ofrenda" in glowing gold, then one headline: "Light a candle for what still lives in memory." Then one short lede about digital altar + Día de los Muertos + open-weight Qwen. Then two pill CTAs only:
     • Primary filled marigold gradient: "Build an ofrenda" (tiny flame dot on the left)
     • Ghost outline: "See the altar wall"
   - Right: the wooden altar shelf visual with red papel picado on top, 5 lit candles, 3 marigolds. Edge-to-edge atmospheric background — no inset photo cards, no floating badges, no stats.

3) DIVIDER
   - Thin gold line with centered italic serif phrase: "the veil thins here"

4) DEDICATE
   - Eyebrow "DEDICATE", heading "Place your offering", one short supporting line.
   - Glass/dark form panel (not a heavy SaaS card): Name input; kind chips (Person / Dead language / Abandoned project / Forgotten craft / Place); Years optional; Memory textarea; primary button "Light the candle".

5) REMEMBRANCE RESULT (shown as a success state)
   - Centered tall lit candle, small uppercase "REMEMBRANCE", serif title, warm prose body, label "ON THE ALTAR" + pill offering chips, italic centered whisper quote, meta "qwen-3.8-27b · 420 ms on Cerebras", ghost button "Keep on the altar wall".

6) ALTAR WALL
   - Eyebrow "KEEP", heading "Altar wall", one line: candles stay in this browser.
   - Grid of 3 remembrance tiles (interaction containers): mini candle, title, 3–4 lines of text, kind label. Empty state: dashed border italic "No candles yet…"

7) FOOTER
   - Soft crimson papel picado strip, then "Ofrenda · Ghost in the Machine · open-weight Qwen 3.8-27B on Cerebras" and "#figma #hacktoberfest #makeathon"

MOBILE:
- Stack hero copy above altar shelf. Full-width CTAs and form. Keep brand huge. Reduce petal count visually.

MOTION NOTES (for prototype / Make):
- Soft candle flame flicker, slow drifting petals, gentle amber glow breathe. Calm, not noisy.

Deliver polished clickable frames for Desktop and Mobile matching this UI/UX exactly.
```
